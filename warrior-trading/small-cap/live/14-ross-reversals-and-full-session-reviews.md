## Reversal 最小定义

```text
extended decline
→ selling pressure slows
→ failed new low / higher low
→ key level reclaim
→ retest holds
→ entry with structural invalidation
```

越早入场，价格越好但证据越少；越晚确认，胜率可能更高但止损/盈亏空间会变化。两者应作为不同策略分别统计。
